Branding / Digital

A Marseille based startup building haptic technology for XR and VR, bringing synchronized touch into immersive experiences. The technology is extraordinary, but the brand read like a prototype. When what you sell is feeling, the identity has to make people want to reach out and touch.

( Concept )
So we rebuilt the identity around that exact promise: touch made tangible. Not another sterile tech-startup skin, but a system with texture, presence, and warmth, something that signals the human sensation at the core of the product before a single word is read. An identity that doesn't just describe haptics, it makes you feel them. The forgotten sense, finally branded.
